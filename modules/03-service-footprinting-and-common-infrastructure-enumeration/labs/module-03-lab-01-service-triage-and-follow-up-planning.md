# Module 03 Lab 01 - Service Triage and Follow-Up Planning

---

> **🛠 Lab Objective**
>
> Turn a host's service exposure into a role classification, evidence summary, and prioritized next-step plan.

| Module | Position | Main output |
|---|---|---|
| Module 03 - Service Footprinting and Common Infrastructure Enumeration | End-of-module lab | service-role notes, captured nouns, triage queue, workflow routing decisions |

| Prerequisites | Use while working |
|---|---|
| Lessons 3.1-3.4 | [field reference](../references/module-03-reference-cheat-sheet.md), [service role matrix](../references/module-03-service-role-matrix.md) |

---

## Planned Lab Structure

This lab will be implemented as a guided triage exercise with:

1. service mix review
2. host-role classification
3. evidence capture
4. cross-service correlation
5. priority scoring
6. next-workflow routing

---

## Expected Learner Artifacts

- service-role classification
- host-role hypothesis
- exact nouns captured from service output
- highest-priority follow-up paths
- open questions and deferred paths

---

## Design Notes

When fully implemented, this lab should feel like a small analyst workshop rather than a protocol checklist.
